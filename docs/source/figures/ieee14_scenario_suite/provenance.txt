Pure cache reader. Nothing is simulated, re-solved, smoothed, filtered, decimated,
  clipped, offset, interpolated or padded: every plotted sample is a raw accepted
  value from the stored trajectory.

Severity is MASKED to NaN wherever a converter is out of service. The engine forms
  S = min(1,max(0,0.5*J_V+0.5*J_f)) and the overlay publishes J_V = J_f = NaN for an
  offline device; because max(0,NaN) is 0 in MATLAB, an unmasked panel would draw a
  tripped converter as the calmest device in the island. P and Q are NOT masked: a
  converter out of service injects no current, so its zero is measured.

The x axis spans the REQUESTED horizon on every page. A run that stopped early is
  marked with a red validity rule at its last accepted sample instead of having the
  axis cropped to its data.

Gamma_on / Gamma_off are read per page from the option signature stored in the cache,
  not defaulted in the generator: they are top-level run options and are not
  republished inside the result.

Vertical rules are unlabelled ON the page -- four tiles about 2.2 in wide leave no
  room for event names without overprinting -- and are distinguished by line style:
  dotted = scheduled disturbance, dash-dot = supervisor commitment (dotted grey when
  refused), solid red = validity exit. Every rule is listed under its page below, with its
  time, its name and the result field it was read from.
