\begin{abstract}
One six-state EMF6 synchronous generator (SG) and four registered full-state dual-mode IBRs are
simulated by the project-owned fixed-step, all-KCL hybrid engine for 160~s. The exact chronology is
SG trip at 20~s, load increase at 50~s, bus-9 fault at 85--85.15~s, line 6--13 trip at 110~s,
topology restoration and SG-close request at 145~s, and endpoint at 160~s. The run converges with
maximum accepted-step residual $9.98\times10^{-9}$ and at most one subdivision level. The synchronism
guard accepts the SG at 147.175~s; all IBRs then return from GFM to GFL through the separately declared
coordinated SG-reference handback. Final SG frequency is 60.0468~Hz and is still settling. No
Bayesian-optimisation controller is used. A seeded band-limited display ripple is drawn beside,
not instead of, each raw trace and affects figures only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Case, decision index, and switching semantics}
The system base is 100~MVA and 60~Hz. The SG is at bus 1; IBR1--IBR4 are at buses 2, 3, 6, and 8.
The project AGSI++ index is
\begin{align}
J_V&=\frac{|V-V_{ref}|}{0.10}, & J_f&=\frac{|f-60|}{0.50}, &
J_R&=\frac{|\mathrm df/\mathrm dt|}{1.0},\\
J_P&=\frac{|P_{ref}-P|}{0.20}, &
J_{SCR}&=\max(0,3/SCR-1), & J_{lock}&=\frac{|v_q|}{0.10},\\
J_{GRA}&=1-GRA, &
\mathrm{AGSI}^{++}_i&=\min\!\left(1,\max\!\left(0,\frac{1}{7}\sum J_k\right)\right).
\end{align}
Here $GRA=1$ when an online SG or committed GFM supplies a reference. The generic local state
machine uses $\Gamma_{on}=0.65$, $\Gamma_{off}=0.35$, $T_{d,on}=0.10$~s, and
$T_{d,off}=1.00$~s. In this declared chronology, however, the SG-trip transaction is a
\texttt{CASE\_DEFINED} all-GFM override, and the return at SG close is a coordinated handback.
Neither transaction is presented as an index-threshold crossing.

\section{Dynamic equations}
The operational SG state is
$x_{SG}=[\delta,\Delta\omega,E'_q,E'_d,E''_q,E''_d]^T$:
\begin{align}
\dot\delta&=\omega_0\Delta\omega,&
2H\dot{\Delta\omega}&=P_m-P_e-D\Delta\omega,\\
T'_{d0}\dot E'_q&=E_{fd}+c_dE''_q-d_dE'_q,&
T'_{q0}\dot E'_d&=c_qE''_d-d_qE'_d,\\
T''_{d0}\dot E''_q&=E'_q-E''_q-(X'_d-X''_d)I_d,&
T''_{q0}\dot E''_d&=E'_d-E''_d+(X'_q-X''_q)I_q.
\end{align}
The source singular limit $T'_{q0}=0$ freezes $E'_d=0$ exactly. Two prime-mover states remain
outside the six-state electromagnetic order:
\[
T_{SV}\dot P_{SV}=-P_{SV}+P_c-\Delta\omega/R,\qquad
T_{CH}\dot P_m=-P_m+P_{SV}.
\]
They provide offline runback/phase preparation and post-close primary regulation. The numerical
mapping is diagnostic and is not a machine-specific governor validation.

Each IBR retains the explicit 20-state switching superset
\begin{align}
x_{GFM}={}&[\Delta\omega_m,\delta_{IT},x_w,x_E,\delta_{PLL},x_{PLL},P_{inv,f},
I_{d,f},Q_{inv,f},V_{inv,f},I_{q,f},\delta_{IT,max},\delta_{IT,min}]^T,\\
x_{GFL}={}&[V_{t,f},P_f,I_{q,cmd,f},P_{ord},V_{LVPL,f},I_{p,reg},I_{q,reg}]^T.
\end{align}
The GFM branch contains the sourced VSM swing, PLL, voltage integrator, five measurement filters,
and dynamic current-angle bounds; for example
\begin{align}
2H\dot{\Delta\omega}_m&=P_{ref,inv}-P_{inv,f}-(1/m_p+D_1)\Delta\omega_m
-D_2(\Delta\omega_m-x_w),\\
T_{pf}\dot P_{inv,f}&=\kappa P-P_{inv,f},&
T_{Vf}\dot V_{inv,f}&=|V|-V_{inv,f}.
\end{align}
The GFL branch uses the registered WECC REGC\_A and REEC\_A models. Its states represent
voltage and power filters, power order, LVPL, and regulated-current dynamics. Only the active branch enters the dynamic solve. Atomic state
mapping is followed by a full-KCL right-limit solve; a failed solve rolls the transaction back.

At the accepted close, the synchronism mismatch is $\Delta V=0.01277$~pu,
$\Delta f=7.796\times10^{-4}$~pu, and $\Delta\theta=3.402^\circ$. At 160~s the bus-voltage
range is 0.9849--1.0556~pu, SG electrical power is 1.0482~pu, mechanical input is 1.0462~pu,
and SG frequency is 60.0468~Hz with a nonzero settling slope. Thus the trajectory demonstrates
a successful declared handback, but not exact steady-state restoration at the endpoint.

\section{Evidence boundary and reproduction}
The primary run uses $\Delta t=0.0125$~s. A $\Delta t=0.025$~s refinement comparison closes at
148.175~s rather than 147.175~s, so event-time independence is not claimed. The run proves numerical
execution of the declared chronology, atomic mode transfer, full KCL,
synchronism-gated close, and return to all GFL. It does not validate the diagnostic synchronizer,
governor parameters, energy reserve, or a general protection design against field measurements.
The dotted display ripple is never used as model evidence; the solid raw traces remain visible.
