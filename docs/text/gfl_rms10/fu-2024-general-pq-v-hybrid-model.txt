                                         VI. CONCLUSION
                To analyze the power, voltage, and frequency oscillation or
             instability of a multi-(VSC) system, a general [P Q]-[ω V] model
             for a multi-VSC system under arbitrary structure is established.
             The system allows both grid-forming and grid-following VSCs,
             which provides a new power-system-based oscillation and sta-
             bility analysis standard model for hybrid GFM/GFL VSCs sys-
             tem. Based on the [P Q]-[ω V] model, the weak nodes with severe
             low-frequency and medium-frequency (a few tenths to tens Hz)
             power oscillations are located and the effect of the oscillation
             suppression method is evaluated. Besides, the proposed WFCF
