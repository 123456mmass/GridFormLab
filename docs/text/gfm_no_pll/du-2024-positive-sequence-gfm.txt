                                                                As shown in Fig. 3(a), when Pf exceeds Pmax or drops below

                                                             inverter output voltage [35]. The active power and reactive power
                                                             limiting controls can be added either on the direct droop control
                                                             or on the multi-loop droop control.

                                                             C. Current Limiting During Faults
                                                                Because a GFM approximately behaves as a voltage source
                                                             behind impedance during normal operations, during a severe
                                                             short-circuit fault it can generate high currents with the pos-
                                                             sibility of damaging the inverter hardware. Therefore, current
                                                             limiting controls are needed to prevent GFMs from overcurrent
                                                             during faults. Considering the characteristic of insulated-gate
                                                             bipolar transistors (IGBTs), the overcurrent needs to be limited
                                                             rapidly, typically within 1 ms as reported by the inverter man-
                                                             ufacturer [36]. This subsection introduces two current limiting
                                                             controls that are typically used by the direct droop and multi-loop
                                                             droop controls.
                                                                For the direct droop control, since it does not have an inner
                                                             current loop, the current limiting is implemented at the PWM
                                                             control layer. The typical approach is to block the associated
                                                             IGBTs once detecting the overcurrent, and then release the
                                                             blocking signal once the current drops below the threshold Imax
                                                             using a hysteresis loop. Fig. 4 shows the current limiting control
                                                             block for phase A. Once the output current ila exceeds Imax ,
                                                             the hysteresis loop sends the blocking signal blka to IGBT 1
                                                             and IGBT 4 of the phase A bridge, where g1 and g4 are the
                                                             original gate signals generated by the PWM control, and g’ 1
