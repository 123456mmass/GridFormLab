    Virtual Synchronous Generator without Phase Locked Loop based on
            Current Controlled Inverter and its Parameter Design
                   Kenichi Sakimoto∗a)                   Member,                Kazushige Sugimoto∗                    Member

           The inverters which simulate the actual synchronous generators can continue to operate in grid disconnection. It
        is called virtual synchronous generator (VSG). In this paper, the VSG without phase locked loop (PLL) based on a
        current controlled inverter is proposed. The proposed controller is composed of impedance model, governor, AVR,
        damper model and rotor model. It can be synchronized with grid without PLL because it has synchronizing power.
        VSG parameter design method is provided by stability analysis. Finally, simulation and experimental results show that
        the proposed VSG can operate in disconnection from the grid. It can supply load after disconnection from grid.

        Keywords: virtual synchronous generator, grid connected inverter, distributed generator, grid disconnection
